## Comparison

Over the same full months (January to September), 2026 is about 11% behind 2025: 324 new contacts against 363.

Most of the gap comes from April and May, with 56 contacts in 2026 against 95 in 2025.

September 2026 was well ahead of last year, with 51 against 37.

October 2026 has 22 contacts after 19 days. At that pace it would end the month at about 36, in line with the 2026 average.

November and December were among the strongest months of 2025, at 55 and 54. If that repeats, 2026 could close much of the gap.
